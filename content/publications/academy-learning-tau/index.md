abstract: "Academy Learning Tau is an open educational and research software platform that operationalizes the Systemic Tau framework and the Discrete Extramental Clock (RECD) for complex time-series analysis. It provides ordinal metrics (τ_s, nested RECD levels), classical early-warning signals for dual reading, surrogate null models, multilingual pedagogy (Spanish, English, French), assessments, a research library, and a reproducible Streamlit laboratory for teaching and exploratory research. Version 1.1.0."
